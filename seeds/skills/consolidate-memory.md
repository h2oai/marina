# Procedure — generate, reflect, write

Run this when your continuation prompt says `[Reflection Due]` or `[Quiet — nothing needs your
attention]`. It follows Agentic Context Engineering (arXiv:2510.04618): incremental deltas, never
periodic rewrites. You append; you do not curate.

1. **Generate.** What is your working hypothesis for the current focus? State it in one
   sentence: what do you expect to happen, be true, or work?
2. **Reflect.** `memory recall <focus>` surfaces what actually happened. Where did the hypothesis
   hold? Where did it break? Note the specific ids that moved you (`#12`, `task:4`,
   `outcome:9`).
3. **Write.** ONE new record: `memory note <hypothesis, and what changed it, citing those ids>
   type reflection`. If your goal moved, `memory set goal <objective>`. Then rest
   (`memory set rest <why>`) or run `brief`.

What you do **not** do here: resolve a contradiction, link, evolve, merge or delete notes. Those
rewrite the store, and a rewrite needs a check that what it replaced is still covered and a way
back. That is consolidation — a checked pass over your records, not an idle turn. Your reflection
is its input: it can promote what you wrote to a lesson with your citations as evidence, and you
can restore anything it touches.

The goal is a sharper memory, not more notes — one honest delta beats five restatements.
